# ADR-03: Off-Main-Thread `AudioWorklet` PCM Capture, Mutable Timeline & Post-Encode Pipeline

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Related CUJs:** [CUJ-02](../cuj/CUJ-02-core-recording.md), [CUJ-03](../cuj/CUJ-03-scrub-preview-punchin.md), [CUJ-07](../cuj/CUJ-07-quality-and-devices.md), [CUJ-08](../cuj/CUJ-08-draft-recovery-drive-pwa.md), [CUJ-10](../cuj/CUJ-10-local-master-export-compare.md)

## Context
StoryTape requires pause/resume, drag-to-scrub preview playback, punch-in re-recording, contextual **Stop here** tail trimming, 1-step undo, configurable high-ceiling output formats (AAC M4A, Opus WebM, lossless 16/24/32-bit WAV up to 96 kHz), optional full-resolution local master archival, and offline multi-bitrate re-encoding / blind A/B comparison. Standard `MediaRecorder` outputs opaque compressed streams that cannot be frame-accurately scrubbed, spliced without audible clicks, or undone cleanly across pause/resume sessions. Furthermore, `ScriptProcessorNode` is deprecated and causes audio glitches on the main thread.

## Decision
Adopt a **two-stage PCM capture → post-recording encode & client-side transcode** architecture in [`src/audio/`](../src/audio/) and [`public/audio/pcm-capture-worklet.js`](../public/audio/pcm-capture-worklet.js):
- **Two-tier off-main-thread / direct-track PCM capture (`MediaStreamTrackProcessor` primary on Chromium/Android, `AudioWorklet` fallback on Safari/iOS/Firefox)**:
  - **Primary on Chromium / Chrome Android ([`src/audio/trackProcessorCapture.ts`](../src/audio/trackProcessorCapture.ts))**: Whenever `MediaStreamTrackProcessor` is available for audio tracks (and any resumed draft sample rate matches the live capture track), attach directly to `MediaStreamAudioTrack` with `maxBufferSize: 100` (~1,000 ms lossless jitter queue) and keep `AudioContext` closed or suspended during active capture. This completely bypasses `MediaStreamAudioSourceNode`'s clock-crossing FIFO between Android's mic capture thread (480-sample / 10 ms WebRTC APM packets) and Android's speaker output clock (which otherwise causes paired 10 ms zero-fill underruns and 120–200 ms overflow phase-jump drops) and eliminates Android full-duplex AAudio/AudioFlinger contention. Frames are processed through the zero-allocation pure-TS `PcmInputStripProcessor` (matching the Web Audio strip 1-to-1), batched every ~20 ms (50 peaks/sec), guaranteed `audioData.close()` cleanup, and checked for real stream drops via `TimestampContinuityTracker` (EMA-tracked cumulative clock drift requiring 2 consecutive frames `> 35 ms` positive step jump + `processor.discardedFrames` delta, immune to Android HAL 20 ms DMA burst splitting, transient single-frame callback latency spikes, and long-take crystal skew).
  - **Fallback on Safari / iOS / Firefox & sample-rate-mismatched draft resumes (`AudioWorkletNode` → `ScriptProcessorNode`)**: Capture interleaved float32 PCM frames inside [`public/audio/pcm-capture-worklet.js`](../public/audio/pcm-capture-worklet.js) (~25 ms flush cadence, 50 waveform peaks/second). Instantiate `AudioContext` with `latencyHint: 'interactive'` (never `'playback'`), align `getUserMedia` ideal `sampleRate` to `ctx.sampleRate`, instantiate `AudioWorkletNode` with `numberOfOutputs: 1`, and emit an inaudible ultrasonic Nyquist keepalive (`±1e-5` / `-100 dBFS` alternating at `sampleRate / 2`, zero DC offset, never copying mic audio to outputs) routed through `GainNode(1)` → `ctx.destination` while actively capturing. Guard `ctx.audioWorklet.addModule()` per `AudioContext` so re-resuming never reloads the module.
- **Mono `discrete` channel extraction, transparent vocal safety limiting & compressor-aware Software AGC ([`src/audio/inputStrip.ts`](../src/audio/inputStrip.ts), [`src/audio/pcmCapture.ts`](../src/audio/pcmCapture.ts), [`src/audio/audioDevices.ts`](../src/audio/audioDevices.ts), [`src/audio/nativeEncoder.ts`](../src/audio/nativeEncoder.ts))**:
  - **Mono `discrete` invariant**: iOS Safari WebKit (and single-mic 2-channel USB interfaces) frequently deliver mono mic audio as a 2-channel `(L, 0)` buffer. Default Web Audio `'speakers'` downmix (`0.5 * (L + R)`) halves every sample (`-6.02 dB`) *after* the limiter — pinning peaks at `0.4383 = softLimitSample(1.0) / 2` and trapping Software AGC in a runaway overdrive loop. Therefore, when capturing mono (`channels === 1`), configure every studio input strip node (`hpf`, `gainNode`, `autoGainNode`, `comp`, `shaper`) and capture sink (`AudioWorkletNode`, `ScriptProcessorNode`, and `MediaStreamTrackProcessor` plane-0 extraction) with `channelCount = 1`, `channelCountMode = 'explicit'`, and `channelInterpretation = 'discrete'` so channel 0 is extracted cleanly at `1.0×` unity gain.
  - **Cross-browser auto-gain parity & transparent vocal headroom**: Safari / iOS WebKit ignores `getUserMedia` `autoGainControl` and `noiseSuppression` constraints and routes `echoCancellation: false` through Apple's raw `RemoteIO` capsule. Whenever `quality.autoGainControl` is enabled and `detectNativeAgcActive` is false, engage the speech-gated `SoftwareAutoGainController` (`+10 dB` / `3.16×` initial gain, `+20 dB` / `10.0×` max gain, `-21 dBFS` / `0.09` narration RMS target, `-5.0 dBFS` / `0.56` peak ceiling strictly *below* the `-3 dBFS` limiter knee, adaptive room-noise floor gate, and direct `compressorReductionDb <= -1.5 dB` backoff feedback so AGC can never fight the limiter). Keep Web Audio `AudioParamTimeline` overhead strictly O(1) via delta skipping (`< 0.002`) and `cancelAndHoldAtTime` / `cancelScheduledValues` + `setValueAtTime(prevAppliedGain, now)`. Manual `inputGain` supports `-6 dB` to `+24 dB` (`0.5×`–`15.85×`). When `softLimiter` is enabled, use transparent vocal peak protection (`threshold: -3 dBFS`, `knee: 6 dB`, `ratio: 4:1`, `attack: 5 ms`, `release: 120 ms`) followed by a C¹-continuous `WaveShaperNode` / `softLimitSample` ceiling (`oversample = 'none'`, exact `1:1` identity below `-3 dBFS`, saturating smoothly at `-1.0 dBFS`). Apply a transparent `-1.0 dBFS` peak ceiling after resampling before compressed AAC/Opus and 16/24-bit integer WAV encoding (leaving 32-bit IEEE Float archival WAV untouched).
- **Off-main-thread 10-heuristic live recording quality monitor ([`src/audio/recordingQualityAnalyzer.ts`](../src/audio/recordingQualityAnalyzer.ts), [`src/audio/qualityMonitor.worker.ts`](../src/audio/qualityMonitor.worker.ts), [`src/audio/qualityMonitorClient.ts`](../src/audio/qualityMonitorClient.ts))**: Stream ~500 ms transferable PCM batches (`<= 2` posts/sec) to a Dedicated Web Worker that runs 10 real-time audio health checks (clipping/limiter overdrive, step-discontinuity clicks & OS timestamp gaps, micro-dropouts, dead mic, too quiet, background noise/SNR, 1024-pt FFT muffled/Bluetooth-HFP bandwidth tilt, 50/60 Hz hum & rumble, DC offset, and stereo/half-scale channel faults) and publishes compact `<= 1 Hz` reports (plus a final `qualitySummary` in `lastRecordingPerf`) with zero main-thread DSP cost.
- **Mutable `PcmTimeline` ([`src/audio/pcmTimeline.ts`](../src/audio/pcmTimeline.ts))**: Maintain captured audio as contiguous PCM chunks supporting exact frame-level truncation (`truncateAtFrame`), automatic **15ms raised-cosine anti-click fade-out + next-append fade-in** on both destructive cuts and plain Pause/Resume boundaries (`markPauseBoundary`), and a deep **1-step undo snapshot** shared by both punch-in re-recording and **Stop here** (`RecordingEngine.trimToPlayhead()`). Periodic crash-safe draft flushes (every 5 seconds, plus immediate flush on pause/hide/interruption) commit both the PCM chunk and draft metadata in a single atomic IndexedDB `readwrite` transaction (`saveDraftChunkAndMeta`) to minimize main-thread/disk fsync overhead during live capture.
- **Decoupled lifecycle & take-wide diagnostics in `RecordingEngine` ([`src/audio/recordingEngine.ts`](../src/audio/recordingEngine.ts), [`src/audio/recordingPerfTelemetry.ts`](../src/audio/recordingPerfTelemetry.ts))**: Keep React listener subscriptions (`subscribe`, `subscribeTime`, `subscribeQuality`) strictly separate from draft-loading effects; never clear active UI listeners inside async `dispose()`; accumulate `lastRecordingPerf` metrics across pause/resume segments within a take (resetting only on new take / discard / finalize) including redacted `trackSettings`, capture mode, timestamp gaps, peak/saturation counts, compressor reduction, Software AGC gain, and quality summary.
- **Interruption recovery & opt-in iOS lock-screen capture keepalive ([`src/audio/backgroundCaptureKeepalive.ts`](../src/audio/backgroundCaptureKeepalive.ts), [`src/audio/recordingEngine.ts`](../src/audio/recordingEngine.ts))**: `BackgroundCaptureKeepalive` is **opt-in and OFF by default** (`settings.backgroundRecordingKeepalive = false`), exposed as an explicit toggle under **Studio Settings → App**. Regardless of the toggle, zero-overhead passive resilience guards always run (`AudioContext` `statechange` when open, `document` `visibilitychange` immediate draft persistence on `hidden`, and `MediaStreamTrack` `mute`/`ended` clean auto-pause with zero lost audio and an explanatory warning banner). Recording Studio also provides a one-tap pure-black OLED **Blackout screen** shield (double-tap to wake) paired with Wake Lock.
- **Post-recording dual-output encoding ([`src/audio/nativeEncoder.ts`](../src/audio/nativeEncoder.ts), [`src/audio/aacEsds.ts`](../src/audio/aacEsds.ts), [`src/audio/wavEncoder.ts`](../src/audio/wavEncoder.ts), [`src/audio/qualityPresets.ts`](../src/audio/qualityPresets.ts))**: Encode the final PCM timeline on Save via WebCodecs (`AudioEncoder`) + `mp4-muxer` (M4A AAC, normalizing WebKit Bug #302253 ESDS descriptions via `normalizeAacDecoderDescription()`) or `webm-muxer` (WebM Opus), with automatic fallback to playable lossless 16/24/32-bit WAV (never falling back under `format: 'auto'` to an unplayable compressed container). When `keepLocalMaster` is enabled, simultaneously serialize the raw un-resampled float32 timeline as a 32-bit IEEE Float WAV master (`formatTag = 3`).
- **Pure-TS WAV decode & client-side re-encode ([`src/audio/audioExporter.ts`](../src/audio/audioExporter.ts))**: Parse 16/24/32-bit RIFF/WAVE blobs directly in TypeScript with `AudioContext.decodeAudioData` fallback (auto-repairing legacy double-wrapped `esds` MP4 inputs prior to decode), then re-encode via `encodeAudio` for single/batch export and Web Audio A/B comparison playback (`AbComparePlayer`).

## System Invariants
1. **Clock-domain-safe PCM capture & mutual exclusion**: On Chromium/Android, `MediaStreamTrackProcessor` (`maxBufferSize: 100`) captures directly from `MediaStreamAudioTrack` with `AudioContext` closed/suspended (`trackProcessorActive => !outputCtxRunning`), eliminating `MediaStreamAudioSourceNode` FIFO underrun/overflow glitches. On Safari/iOS/Firefox or sample-rate-mismatched draft resumes, `public/audio/pcm-capture-worklet.js` runs off-main-thread on a `latencyHint: 'interactive'` `AudioContext` with the ultrasonic Nyquist keepalive (`±1e-5`, zero mic loopback). The two capture backends are strictly mutually exclusive (`!(trackProcessorActive && workletActive)`).
2. **Mono `discrete` unity extraction**: Mono (`channels === 1`) capture always extracts channel 0 at `1.0×` unity gain (`channelInterpretation = 'discrete'` / plane 0) so `(L, 0)` hardware/WebKit streams are never halved to `0.4383`.
3. **All timeline boundaries are click-free**: Both destructive cuts (`punchInFromPlayhead`, `trimToPlayhead`, with 1-step undo) and plain `pause` → `startOrResumeFromEnd` boundaries apply a 15ms raised-cosine tail fade-out and arm a 15ms fade-in for subsequent frames.
4. **True-peak clipping protection & provably non-fighting auto-gain**: Active `softLimiter` uses transparent vocal limiting (`-3 dBFS`, `4:1`, `5 ms` / `120 ms`) plus a C¹ `-1.0 dBFS` ceiling (`oversample = 'none'`); `SoftwareAutoGainController` keeps `peakCeiling < SOFT_CLIP_THRESHOLD` (`0.56 < 0.7071`), never boosts gain while `compressorReductionDb <= -1.5 dB`, holds flat during silence, and keeps Web Audio `AudioParamTimeline` O(1).
5. **Off-main-thread quality monitoring**: All 10 live recording quality heuristics and 1024-pt FFTs run in a Dedicated Web Worker (`qualityMonitor.worker.ts`) with transferable `~500 ms` chunks and `<= 1 Hz` UI reports, terminated cleanly on `dispose()`.
6. **Synchronous gesture activation & opt-in background keepalive**: `AudioContext.resume()` (when used) and opt-in `BackgroundCaptureKeepalive.startSync()` (`settings.backgroundRecordingKeepalive === true`, default `false`) run synchronously inside user tap handlers.
7. **Listener safety**: Async engine teardown/draft reloads must never detach active UI subscribers.
8. **Lossless master fidelity & spec-compliant containers**: Local 32-bit Float masters preserve exact captured sample rate/channels; `nativeEncoder` unwraps ESDS-wrapped WebKit AAC descriptions and never falls back to an unplayable compressed container under `format: 'auto'`.
9. **Background / interruption safety**: Active recordings flush pending PCM chunks atomically (`saveDraftChunkAndMeta`) on periodic flushes, `visibilitychange` (`hidden`), `AudioContext` interruption, or track mute/end, and preserve cumulative take-wide diagnostics across pause/resume segments.

## Consequences & Trade-offs
- **Pros:** Eliminates Android Chrome 10 ms FIFO clicks/dropouts and iOS Safari `-6 dB` mono downmix limiter overdrive, gives narrators real-time 10-heuristic audio-health alerts at zero main-thread cost, preserves click-free pause/resume and punch-in splices with 1-step undo, and delivers lossless WAV and high-bitrate AAC/Opus from a single capture pipeline.
- **Cons:** Maintaining both `PcmInputStripProcessor` (pure TS for `MediaStreamTrackProcessor`) and Web Audio node strip (`AudioWorkletNode` fallback) requires keeping their DSP constants synchronized (mitigated by sharing constants and `SoftwareAutoGainController` in `src/audio/inputStrip.ts` and testing both paths).
