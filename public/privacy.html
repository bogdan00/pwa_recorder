Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Overview

StoryTape is a client-side Progressive Web App (PWA) for recording, organizing, and listening to audiobooks. StoryTape has no backend server, does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and does not sell or share your personal data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All audiobook projects, cover images, chapter audio recordings, playback positions, and app settings are stored locally on your device in your browser's IndexedDB and localStorage.

Microphone access is used solely when you actively record audio or test your microphone level inside the app. Audio is processed locally in your browser and is never streamed to any third-party server.

3. Google Drive Integration

If you choose to connect your Google account, StoryTape uses Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0) directly from your browser to request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope so family collaborators can recursively sync audiobooks, covers, and chapters inside shared Google Drive folders.

  • What StoryTape accesses: StoryTape only creates, reads, and updates your configured StoryTape audiobook root folder and any shared audiobook folders you explicitly link or import (e.g., project.json, cover.jpg, and chapter audio files). StoryTape never scans, modifies, or transmits unrelated files in your Google Drive.
  • Authentication tokens: OAuth tokens are stored only in your browser's localStorage on your device and are revoked and cleared when you sign out.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy: StoryTape's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Your Control & Data Deletion

You can delete local projects and recordings at any time inside StoryTape or by clearing site data in your browser. You can disconnect Google Drive inside Settings → Google Drive and revoke StoryTape's access anytime from your Google Account Permissions.

5. Contact

StoryTape is an open-source project hosted on GitHub. For questions or privacy requests, please open an issue at github.com/bogdan00/pwa_recorder.
